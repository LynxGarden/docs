Checkpoint **CP8** · App: `features/calendar` + `core/integrations` · Backend
migrations: `bookings`, `booking_rpcs`, `health_logs`.

The client **Calendar** tab has three segments:

## Book (realtime)

1-hour slots **06:00–22:00**, **current week only**, **max 10 per slot**, **one
booking per client per day**, cancellable **up to 2h before**. Each slot shows the
**remaining places** (never who booked) and updates live as others book/cancel
(realtime on `bookings`). Booking also **auto-assigns the client's next uncompleted
session** in their current phase and records the assigned coach.

- `book_training(slot_start)` / `cancel_booking(booking)` are SECURITY DEFINER RPCs
  that enforce every rule server-side; the table is read-only (tenant-wide) for live
  capacity. A coach can `reset_assignment_phase(assignment_phase)` to restart a
  client's phase.
- "Add to calendar" pushes a booking to the device calendar (`device_calendar`).

## Activity (health + reminders)

In-app logging of **sleep / meal / activity / weight / water** (`health_logs`,
special-category per GDPR), plus **import** from Apple Health / Google Fit
(`health`) and **Strava** (OAuth). **Reminders** (`reminders`) schedule local
notifications (`flutter_local_notifications`) for weight/water etc.

## History

The client's finished trainings (completed sessions).

## Native integrations (`core/integrations`)

`HealthSyncService`, `StravaService`, `DeviceCalendarService`,
`LocalNotificationsService` — all guarded so the app degrades gracefully when a
capability is unavailable. Platform permissions: iOS HealthKit entitlement +
`NSHealth*`/`NSCalendars*` usage strings; Android Health Connect queries,
calendar + `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` permissions, `FlutterFragmentActivity`, minSdk 26.
Strava needs `STRAVA_CLIENT_ID/SECRET/REDIRECT_URI` env + the deep-link redirect.
