Checkpoint **CP7** · App: `features/progress` · Backend: none new (aggregates the
existing `logged_entries` + `assignment_exercises`, client-readable under RLS).

The client **Progress** tab turns their logged history into feedback:

- **Streak / visits** — distinct training days in the current week, plus a
  consecutive-active-weeks streak.
- **Personal bests** (per exercise) — best weight, best reps, and best **day**
  volume: the sum of reps × weight across *all sets* logged that day, best day
  wins (consistent with the weekly volume chart — it is no longer a single set's
  reps × weight).
- **Volume** — per-session volume = Σ(reps × weight); shown as a weekly series.
- **Graph** — an `fl_chart` line chart of weekly volume / top weight over time, with
  a Volume ⇄ Best-weight toggle. Fully theme-driven (light + dark).

## Data

No new tables. Aggregation is **client-side** in `ProgressRepository` over the
client's own `logged_entries` (reps/weight/time/`data`, keyed on `exercise_id`). The
tracked-exercise picker lists distinct `(exercise_id, exercise_name)` from the
client's `assignment_exercises` snapshots (clients can't read the `exercises` table).

## App

`ProgressScreen` (tab root) with stat tiles, PB tiles, exercise picker and the
chart; layout-matching skeleton; empty states. `progressSummaryProvider` +
`trackedExercisesProvider` + a per-exercise series provider.
