## The account-less client

The domain identity is a **`person`** (see [Data model](/docs/backend/data-model)).
A person may or may not link to an `auth.users` account: **a client record is not
a user account**. Elderly / 1-on-1 PT clients never open the app — staff operate
their profile, program, and history on their behalf.

## Safety flags — special-category data

**`health_flags`** stores coach-side safety screening (smoking, blood pressure,
vertigo, migraines, medications, allergies, asthma, osteoporosis, joint pain,
spine condition, other).

<Callout type="warn">
`health_flags` is **special-category personal data (GDPR)**. The person can read
their own; staff read + write the tenant's. It is never exposed cross-tenant.
</Callout>

## Invites → binding an account to a person

Onboarding: staff add the client → send an **invite** → client downloads the app,
signs up, and their **new account binds to the pre-existing person row**.

**`invites`**: `code` (unique), `person_id`, `status` (`pending` / `accepted` /
`expired`), `expires_at`.

### `redeem_invite(code)` — RPC

`SECURITY DEFINER` (a freshly signed-up user has no person/tenant yet). It looks
up a pending, unexpired invite, sets `users_data.auth_user_id = auth.uid()` on the
target person **only if still unlinked**, and marks the invite `accepted`.

## App

Clients tab: list, add account-less client, detail (safety flags, assignments,
**Assign** via template picker, **Invite** → code). Invite redemption runs on the
client's finish-setup step.
